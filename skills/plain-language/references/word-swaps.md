## Doublets and triplets

Cut repeated words that mean the same thing (federal guide).

| Instead of | Try |
|---|---|
| cease and desist* | stop |
| due and payable* | due |
| any and all | all |
| each and every | each, every |
| null and void | void |

## Modifiers to cut

These rarely add meaning (federal guide): absolutely, actually, completely, really, quite, totally, very.
